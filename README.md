# NOVALITH

> **Explore Mars. Understand the Unknown.**

NOVALITH is a six-person team project for **NASA Space Apps Cairo 2026**. Our challenge, **Interplanetary Survival Guide — Martian Map**, explores how an interactive map could help people understand the Martian landscape and investigate routes across the Red Planet.

## The idea

Mars is vast, varied, and challenging to explore. NOVALITH brings key geographic context into one mapping experience, helping users move from viewing the terrain to asking better questions about where and how to explore.

## What the map brings together

- **Terrain and elevation** to show the shape of the Martian surface.
- **Geological features** to give context to places of interest.
- **Exploration locations** to highlight areas to investigate.
- **Potential hazards** to support route exploration.

These are the project goals. Specific datasets, tools, and implemented features will be documented here as the project develops.
